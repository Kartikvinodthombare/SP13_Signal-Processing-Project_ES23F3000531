📚 Filter Theory Guide:
  • Mean Filter: A simple blur that averages all pixels in the kernel equally. Fast, but can leave blocky artifacts.
  • Gaussian Filter: A natural, smooth blur. It uses a bell-curve weight, meaning the center pixel matters most, and edges fade out gently.
  • Laplacian Filter: A high-pass filter that detects rapid changes in color. It ignores flat areas and highlights borders to find edges or sharpen the image.
